AP Calculus & Chemistry Study Notes — Chapter 4: Integration & Equilibrium

1. Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

If f is continuous on [a, b], then the function F(x) = ∫ax f(t)dt is differentiable on (a, b) and F'(x) = f(x).

2. Chemical Equilibrium Constant (Keq)

For a general reaction aA + bB → cC + dD, the equilibrium expression is given by Keq = [C]c[D]d / ([A]a[B]b).

Note: Pure solids and liquids are omitted from the equilibrium expression.
